      How the schedule is chosen. Every submitted availability window is treated as a hard rule. Within those rules, the tool finds the arrangement with the highest total estimated prep points (speedups count 30 points per minute; Fire Crystals 2,000; Refined Fire Crystals 30,000; Fire Shards 1,000), counting each player's general speedups once across the whole event — then fills as many remaining slots as possible, favoring players with the fewest free times. The math behind it is an exact optimizer, not a rough sort, so no shuffling of appointments could score higher.

      Privacy. The downloaded schedule contains time, player ID, alliance, and (optional) in-game name — never emails. The survey file never leaves this page.
